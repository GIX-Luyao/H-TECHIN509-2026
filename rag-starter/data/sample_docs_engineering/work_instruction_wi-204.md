# WI-204 — Widget Assembly and Torque Verification, Model WA-7 (synthetic training data — not a real document)

> **Synthetic training data.** This work instruction is entirely fictional and was written
> for HMSTI 509 course exercises. Do not use for any real assembly or test activity.

**Document:** WI-204, Rev D
**Applies to:** Widget Assembly WA-7 (7100-WA7-001) per SPEC-1042
**Station:** Bench 12, Assembly Cell 3 (fictional)
**Required training:** WI-204 certification, current calibration awareness briefing

## Tools and materials

- Torque wrench TW-031 (calibrated, 0–15 N·m range)
- Arbor press AP-07 with WA-7 seating fixture FIX-118
- Lint-free gloves, ESD smock
- Lubricant LUB-12 (fictional), applicator pads
- Serialized traveler for the work order

## Safety and cautions

> **CAUTION:** The retention clip (7100-CLP-002) is spring-loaded. Wear safety glasses
> during clip installation and removal. Ejected clips are the most common incident at this
> station.

> **CAUTION:** Do not substitute lubricants. Only LUB-12 is approved on the shaft bore
> (SPEC-1042 REQ-010). Any other lubricant degrades the gasket material.

## Procedure

1. **Verify kit.** Confirm the traveler lists housing, shaft, clip, and gasket lots. If any
   lot number is missing or illegible, STOP and notify the lead — do not proceed.
2. **Inspect housing bore.** Check bore for burrs and FOD under the bench magnifier (2x).
   Wipe with a lint-free pad. Burrs found → write an NCR, set the housing aside.
3. **Install gasket.** Seat gasket 7100-GSK-005 into the housing groove by hand. The gasket
   must sit flush with no twist. Do not use tools on the gasket.
4. **Lubricate shaft.** Apply a thin film of LUB-12 to the shaft bore surface only. One pad
   per shaft. Excess lubricant visible as droplets → wipe and reapply.
5. **Press shaft.** Place the housing in fixture FIX-118 and press the shaft with arbor
   press AP-07 until it bottoms against the internal shoulder. A soft stop is normal; a
   sudden stop before full depth means misalignment — back out and re-inspect.
6. **Install retention clip.** With safety glasses on, seat clip 7100-CLP-002 into its
   groove until it clicks. Verify full seating by rotating the clip ±10 degrees; it must not
   lift out of the groove.
7. **Verify free rotation (REQ-005).** Rotate the shaft by hand. It must turn smoothly with
   no binding. Binding → NCR, do not adjust by force.
8. **Proof torque (REQ-006).** Using TW-031, apply 12 N·m at the coupling and hold for
   5 seconds. Inspect for deformation and gasket extrusion at the interface.
9. **Mark and record.** Apply the serial number label (format WA7-YYWW-NNNN), record the
   torque reading and serial number on the traveler, and initial step 9.
10. **Bag and tag.** Inspect for FOD under 2x magnification, bag the assembly, and attach
    the traveler copy.

## If something goes wrong

Any failed step is documented as a nonconformance report per QP-09 and logged in
`ncr_log.md`. Do not rework without a disposition. When in doubt, stop and ask the lead —
an NCR is cheap; an escape is not.
