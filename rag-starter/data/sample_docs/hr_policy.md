# Acme Inc. — Employee Handbook (synthetic sample data)

## Paid time off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 15 vacation days per year in their first two years, and 20 days
per year after that. Vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the next
calendar year. Submit vacation requests at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not roll over. A doctor's note
is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Fully remote
arrangements require director-level approval and a signed remote-work agreement.

## Expense reimbursement
Submit expenses within 30 days through the finance portal. Receipts are required for any
expense over 25 dollars. Reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle.
