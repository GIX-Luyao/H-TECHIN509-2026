# Acme Cloud — Help Center (synthetic sample data)

## Resetting your password
If you forgot your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter the
email on your account. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 30 minutes. If the
link expires, simply request a new one. Passwords must be at least 12 characters and
include one number.

## Signing in fails with "account locked"
After five failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 15 minutes as a security measure.
Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock immediately.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
We strongly recommend enabling 2FA from Settings → Security. You can use an authenticator
app or SMS. Recovery codes are shown once when you enable 2FA — store them somewhere safe.

## Exporting your data
You can export all of your data as a ZIP file from Settings → Account → Export. Large
exports are emailed to you as a download link when they finish.
