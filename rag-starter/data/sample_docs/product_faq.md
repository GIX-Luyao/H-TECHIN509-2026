# Acme Widget Pro — Product FAQ (synthetic sample data)

## What is Widget Pro?
Widget Pro is a subscription tool for tracking team projects. It includes boards, timelines,
and automated weekly summary reports.

## Pricing
The Starter plan is free for up to 3 users. The Team plan is 8 dollars per user per month and
includes unlimited boards. The Business plan is 16 dollars per user per month and adds
single sign-on (SSO) and audit logs.

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel anytime from Billing → Manage plan. Your plan stays active until the end of
the current billing period, and you are not charged again after that.

## Supported integrations
Widget Pro integrates with Slack, Google Calendar, and GitHub. Integrations are configured
per workspace from Settings → Integrations and require workspace-admin permission.

## Data retention
Deleted projects are kept in the trash for 30 days, after which they are permanently removed
and cannot be recovered.
