# My assistant — scoping brief

Replace the prompts with your own answers. Keep this in `docs/SCOPING.md`.

## Objectives

Who is the user? What should the assistant help them do? List three example questions.

## The gap / need

What happens today? Why is document retrieval appropriate rather than a database query, form, or dashboard?

## Key deliverables

What is the smallest worthwhile version? Name its outputs and one feature you will leave out.

## Data requirements & constraints

Name the synthetic, sanitized, or permitted public sources you will use. State what must never enter the repository or an agent prompt: PII, credentials, employer/client-confidential and export-controlled material. How will you check the boundary?

## Success measure

Give an observable test: questions to ask, expected evidence/citations, a pass threshold, and an unsupported question on which the assistant should say it does not know.

## Peer review and revision

What did your reviewer challenge? Record one concrete before → after revision and why you made it.
