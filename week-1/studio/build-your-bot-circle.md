# Build Your Bot Circle

1. **Share your idea.** Tell a partner who your user is, what information they need, and which safe documents could help.
2. **Challenge the fit.** Would document retrieval help, or would a database query, form, or dashboard be more useful?
3. **Choose a small first version.** List example questions, expected outputs, and one feature you will leave out.
4. **Define success.** Describe a test with a clear pass criterion, including a question the documents cannot answer.
5. **Write your scope.** Fill in the [template](templates/SCOPING.md) and save it as `docs/SCOPING.md`.
6. **Review and revise.** Ask a partner to challenge your success criterion and data boundary. Record one specific change you made after feedback.

Optional AI prompt:

> Ask three questions about my draft scope: an unclear user, an oversized first version, or an untestable success measure. Identify one privacy risk. Do not write the brief for me.

Use only synthetic, sanitized, or permitted public material. Keep PII, credentials, and confidential information out of your documents and prompts.
