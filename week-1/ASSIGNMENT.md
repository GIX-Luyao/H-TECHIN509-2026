# Week 1 assignment

1. Follow the [setup instructions](README.md#setup) and run `python scripts/check_env.py` inside `rag-starter`.
2. Complete the [assignment notebook](HMSTI_509_M1_Assignment.ipynb).
3. Write `docs/SCOPING.md` in your own project: objectives, user/need, smallest version and deliverables, safe data sources, and measurable success. Use the [template](studio/templates/SCOPING.md) or [example](studio/SCOPING_EXAMPLE.md).
4. Add a dated Week 1 entry to `docs/REFLECTIONS.md`: identify two or three text-related tasks at work, choose one for an assistant, and explain what success means.
5. Follow the [Git checklist](studio/GIT_CHECKLIST.md) to push your starter code and documents. Use a public repository or a private one with instructor access.
6. Provide your repository URL, the health check's final line, your reflection, and `shipped: <repository URL>` or `blocked: <specific blocker>` through the course submission channel.

**Assessment:** complete/incomplete, 2% of the course grade. Your scope also counts as Final Project Milestone 0 (5%); draft-quality is acceptable, with revisions through Week 2.

**AI use:** for this independent assignment, use AI to explain or debug; write your own scope and be able to explain your commands. The supervised Arena has its own permissions.

Use only synthetic or sanitized examples. Keep credentials, PII, and confidential material out of code, prompts, and Git.
